## Question

Chapter 09: what exactly is graded in a skills chapter?

CodeCrafters splits skills across six stages (advertise, invoke, arguments,
stack, scripts, model-chooses, subagent). We have one chapter. The real decision
is where the boundary sits: is a skill just markdown injected into the prompt, or
a directory with scripts and arguments? What can be asserted deterministically
when "the model chooses a skill" is finally the model's call?
